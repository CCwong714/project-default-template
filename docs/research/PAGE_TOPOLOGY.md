# Cocota Page Topology

Target viewport references: desktop 1440×900 and mobile 390×844.

1. Fixed navigation
   - Desktop: wordmark, centered links, talk pill, language switcher.
   - Mobile: circular CCT mark, talk pill, menu trigger and fullscreen menu.
2. Hero
   - Four-line oversized claim with two animated team sprites and lime detail dots.
3. Showreel
   - Desktop: 150vh scroll scene with a sticky, full-viewport video.
   - Mobile: a normal 248:176 video card.
4. About and services
   - Top rule and uppercase claim.
   - Large editorial paragraph.
   - Red Branding and blue Web design cards.
   - Supporting sprite, summary, and work call to action.
5. News rail
   - Rule, section label, pagination, horizontally scrollable stories.
6. Selected work
   - Three-line “Strategic creativity since 2017” display heading.
   - Asymmetric 2/3 + 1/3 desktop grid, single column on mobile.
7. Industries and clients
   - “They trust us” display heading and sprite.
   - Industry pills and horizontally scrollable client stories.
8. Awards and studio facts
   - Full-viewport Awards composition with layered trophy imagery.
   - Two-column award list, three facts, and studio photo.
9. Footer
   - “At a glance” heading, company/service links.
   - Newsletter and contact cards.
   - Madrid/London details, social links, funding note, policies.
